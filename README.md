## 🇺🇸 English

> **Frictionless Digital Infrastructure**

We deploy B2B SaaS applications, low-latency corporate VoIP telephony, and complex ERP/CRM integrations. Our mission is to build fast, scalable technology backed by expert strategic support. We believe in high-quality development and actively share our tools with the community.

### ⚙️ Core Tech Stack

* **Backend & Automation:** Python, Laravel, Go, Ansible, Linux
* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript
* **Telecommunications:** Asterisk, OpenSIPS, SIP protocols

### 💡 Areas of Expertise

* **VoIP Networks & Telephony:** Corporate voice systems with minimal latency, SIP trunk configurations, and high cloud security.
* **ERP / CRM Integrations:** Connecting internal systems (SAP, Salesforce, Odoo) to unify databases and streamline business workflows.
* **SaaS & Real-Time Analytics:** Creators of VozInsight (analytics and CDR engine for telephony) and unified telemetry dashboards.

### 🌍 We Build in the Open

Over 10 years creating high-availability solutions. We share utilities, analytics engines, and real-time dashboard components. Explore our repositories, review our code, and contribute!
